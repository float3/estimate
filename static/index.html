Estimation game

For a room of people

How wide is your range?

You ask a question with a number for an answer. Everyone sends a lower and an upper bound from their phone, the room discusses it, and everyone answers again.

How it goes

  1. Everyone scans the join link and gives a name.
  2. You ask a question. Everyone sends the range they are ninety per cent sure the answer falls in.
  3. You stop taking ranges, and the room discusses it.
  4. You ask again. Each phone starts from that player's first answer.
  5. You reveal: the answer, the room's numbers, and what changed between the two passes.
